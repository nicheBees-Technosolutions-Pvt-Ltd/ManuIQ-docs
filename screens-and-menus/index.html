    Screens and menus¶

    This page is generated from the product's screen catalog.

    ManuIQ follows the Infor LN way of working: every screen is a session with its own session code, reached from a menu. Screens without a menu entry open from a related screen (zoom), for example the details of the selected record.

    Session types: Maintain (master data and setups), Update / Process (run a process), Display (inquiries), Workbench, Dashboard. A main session opens from the menu or by zoom; a sub session shows the details of the record selected in its main session.
