    Release notes¶

    ManuIQ is an AI governance platform for manufacturers that run Infor LN. Business agents study your ERP data, recommend improvements, explain their reasoning, and wait for a person to approve before anything changes in Infor LN.

    Version 1.0.0¶

    The first release of ManuIQ. It brings the platform, the administration screens and the first business agent.

    Safety Stock agent¶

    • Reads item, demand and lead-time history from Infor LN for the companies you select.
    • Checks which items qualify for a recommendation and shows why an item was left out.
    • Calculates a recommended safety stock per item using your own policy, and tests it against past demand (backtest) before it is shown.
    • Gives every recommendation a confidence score and a plain-language explanation.
    • Lets planners ask the built-in assistant questions about a recommendation.
    • Planners approve, modify, hold or reject each recommendation, choosing a reason from your own list of reason codes.
    • Approved changes are queued for write-back to Infor LN. Nothing is written to Infor LN without human approval.
    • Safety Stock dashboard with runs, scorecards and decisions.

    Onboarding¶

    • Set up your clients, environments and connections to Infor LN.
    • Load your logistic companies and enterprise structures from Infor LN.
    • Choose which enterprise structures take part, and move each one through its phases (simulation, pilot, live).
    • Number groups and series give every run, approval and audit record a readable number, in the same way as Infor LN.
    • Retention policies control how long records are kept.

    User and role management¶

    • One login per person, with single sign-on and sign-in history.
    • Users are linked to their Infor LN identity in each environment.
    • Roles, role templates, and authorizations per screen, table and data set.
    • Compile authorizations and preview what a user can see before it takes effect.
    • Security dashboard.

    Configuration¶

    • Switch agents on per environment and configure each agent per enterprise structure, including its policy and boundaries.
    • Decide which ERP actions an agent may propose.
    • Maintain your own decision reason codes on top of the standard catalog.
    • Choose the AI model provider, including models hosted on your own servers.
    • Agent configuration and agent fleet dashboards.

    Audit and log¶

    • A complete audit trail of every action, run, approval and AI call.
    • Agent runs with the configuration version they used.
    • Approval drafts, the ERP write-back outbox and its worker, and ERP sync runs.
    • AI model invocations and tokens used.
    • Audit and log dashboard.

    Support and releases¶

    • Software releases, release rollouts per environment and support tickets in the product.
    • Run session: open any screen you may use by its name or code, from the top bar or with Ctrl+R. It is a screen of its own (Tools), so an administrator grants it to a role like any other screen: give the role the Run Session screen and display rights on the Sessions table, then compile the authorizations.

    See Screens and menus for every screen in this release.
